Auto Refresh & Clicker

Help

Refresh on a timer, click buttons by their text, or stop when a keyword appears. Let your browser handle the repeat work.

Enable. Configure. Start.

A short setup, then a clear view of what is running.

  1. 01

    Enable the extension

    Install and open the app, then enable the extension in Safari Settings and allow access to your website.

  2. 02

    Choose your task

    Open the webpage and extension. Choose a mode, interval, and any text to match.

  3. 03

    Stay in control

    Start and check Running and the countdown. Stop at any time, or apply changes to update the task.

iPhone / iPad

In Settings, open Apps → Safari → Extensions. On older systems, use Settings → Safari → Extensions. Enable Auto Refresh & Clicker and allow access to the sites you use.

Mac

Open the installed app, choose Open Safari Settings, and enable Auto Refresh & Clicker in Extensions. Then open a webpage and select its Safari toolbar icon.

Chrome · Desktop

Install from the Chrome Web Store. Open Chrome’s Extensions menu, pin the extension if needed, and open it on the webpage you want to use.

Refresh until your keyword appears, then stop refreshing and highlight the matching text.

On iPhone and iPad, keep Safari in the foreground. The system may suspend tasks in the background or while locked.

A few things to know.

Does it click inside other apps?

Tasks run on webpages in Safari, or in the desktop Chrome extension. It does not control other apps or system interfaces.

Why is the extension missing on my Mac?

Open the installed app at least once, then check Safari Settings → Extensions. Manifest V3 requires Safari 15.4 or later. If it is still missing, contact support with your macOS, Safari, and app versions and installation source.

Will it keep running when my phone is locked?

Continuous background operation is not guaranteed. iOS and iPadOS can suspend browser tasks. Keep Safari in the foreground for active tasks.

Can I change a task while it is running?

In the current Safari version, edit the configuration and choose Apply changes. The summary shows the active task. Choose Stop to end it.
